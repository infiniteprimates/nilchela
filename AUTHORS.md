## Contributors

Human contributors retain the copyright in their own contributions, and are
recorded in the repository's commit history. There is no CLA: contributions are
accepted under the [Developer Certificate of
Origin](CONTRIBUTING.md#sign-off-your-commits-dco), so every human contribution
carries a `Signed-off-by` line naming its author.

## Automation

Part of this project is written by AI agents (the ZeroClaw dev team), working
under the maintainer's authority. **They are tooling, not authors.** They hold
no copyright, they are not contributors for the purposes of attribution, and
they do not sign off — the human certification is the maintainer's, made
through the remediation path described in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md#remediation).

Agent-authored commits disclose themselves with an `Assisted-by:` trailer. A
roster of agent names would be a roster of tool versions; the trailer on the
commit is the record.
