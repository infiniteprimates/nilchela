The volumes are retained, so `helm uninstall` does not delete them. A plain
reinstall afterwards fails on the existing claims — adopt them (storage.<volume>.existingClaim)
or delete them and accept the re-download.

/health on that Service is UNAUTHENTICATED — it reports whether the instance is
paired. Terminate auth before putting anything in front of it.
